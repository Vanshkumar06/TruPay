How TruPay trust score works

The trust score is a confidence signal for a payment. Higher scores mean the payment looks more familiar and consistent with your recent behavior.

85-100
Looks normal for your account, amount, location, and spending rhythm.
70-84
Generally comfortable, with one lighter caution signal that is worth noticing.
45-69
Mostly fine, but something is a little unusual, like a new payee, unusual amount, or different device.
1-44
The payment has multiple warning signals, so TruPay asks for more care before continuing.
